## 5. Key Decisions & Rationale
- **Worktree isolation, NOT a defer-lock (`p74`, P1).** The user's model is 5 subagents in **one** orchestrated session. A single-holder acquire-or-defer lock would idle 4 of 5. Correct fix = per-subagent `git worktree` + collision-free branch + **coexisting** leases + atomic state writes. Do not revert to a mutex.
- **Teardown is its own P1 (`8d0`).** Unattended runs must leave **zero** worktrees/branches/locks behind, even after a crash. `p74` creates isolation; `8d0` *guarantees* reclamation (reaper at preflight + finalize + session-end; commit-dirty-then-remove, never `--force`, never touch a live sibling's lease).
- **`mu3` is the data keystone.** Six beads (`5ft`, `6w8`, `hty`, `hcj`, `dxh`, `xdw`) write into `run-summary.json`. Land `mu3` before them.
- **New beads are the *implementation* of the 5 original *problem* beads — don't double-implement.** Successor mapping: `4zs`→`mu3`; `2e5`→`5ft`+`e1r`; `dcs`→`dxh`; `q5f`→`t6e`+`xdw`; `ohp` stays its own bench track. **Close the original when its successor lands.**
- **Caveat from planning:** a 6-agent assessment Workflow **wedged** here (~26 min idle, agents stalled on a tool-result awaiting a model turn). If you fan out heavy in-session workflows, watch for the same stall; metaswarm's own subagent dispatch is the intended path.
